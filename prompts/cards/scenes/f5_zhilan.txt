Scene: a mist-filled herb valley at early morning, chest-up three-quarter view. The medicine immortal kneels and cups both hands low around a withered seedling, which is unfurling new green leaves into her palms; she watches it with a warm gentle smile, head bent close. The living vines in her hair have begun to bloom in answer. Soft ink-wash cliffs and herb terraces fade into morning fog behind her. Light source: a soft green glow rising from the sprouting seedling in her hands, lighting her face from below-front.
