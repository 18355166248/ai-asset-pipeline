Scene: a cluttered mountain alchemy hut at night, chest-up three-quarter view. The old alchemist leans over a squat bronze cauldron whose lid he has just lifted, catching a single glowing golden pill as it drifts up out of the steam. His eyes crinkle shut and his white beard shakes with a delighted belly laugh. Bundles of dried herbs hang in soft ink-wash blur around him. Light source: the warm golden glow of the pill rising from the cauldron, lighting his face and beard from below-front.
