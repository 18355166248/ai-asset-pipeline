Scene: the open stone platform of a mountain temple in a thunderstorm, chest-up view from slightly below. The thunder-rite daoist stands braced with one foot forward, holding a paper talisman overhead in two fingers as a thread of white lightning strikes down into it, his sleeves and beard blown flat by the wind. His brows are locked in solemn concentration. Ink-wash pines bend behind him. Light source: the white lightning caught in the talisman above him, blasting his upturned face from directly overhead.
